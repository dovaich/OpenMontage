# أم حمادة لايف (Om Hamada Live): low-poly cutscene source

Om Hamada goes live while cooking stuffed cabbage ("three followers... one of them is me").
- She exposes Hamada waking up at 2 PM, and the viewer count explodes.
- Ibrahim barges in, in his undershirt, looking for his red one, then turns TV presenter. #الفانلة_الحمرا trends.
- Everyone forgets the pot, the cabbage burns, and the smoke alarm ends the stream.

- 55.5 s, 4:3, with no episode number. New kitchen set (`kitchen` group, offset to x = 60).
- Most shots are the phone's selfie view. `povCam()` sits in front of Om Hamada's face at arm's length.
  The live UI has the LIVE badge, a viewer count from 3 to 1M, comments and floating hearts.
- The comments carry cameo callbacks: Uncle Sayed, Abu Samir, Hoda, Shehata, and Auntie Soad, who is first to notice the burning cabbage.
- Voices: AI Studio via the Claude-in-Chrome extension (Kore, Puck, Orus, Algenib). The lines were mapped with a `gemini-3.5-flash` transcript.
  A stray sound at the end of the announcer take was cut.
