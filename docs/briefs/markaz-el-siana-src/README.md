# مركز الصيانة (The Service Center): low-poly cutscene source

This episode introduces the service-center cast. Ibrahim flies the Tuk-Tuk 3000 in for "servicing and tuning".
- Bastam (foreman) thinks tuning goes in with the oil.
- El-Alamy (manager) plugs in his laptop, finds "OS: PRESSURE COOKER" and Abu Samir's Wi-Fi, and is annoyed that Ibrahim calls himself "the world-famous inventor", since "El-Alamy" is his own name.
- El-Asly wants to beat whoever wired it with duct tape.
- El-Gazzar fixes it in a second, and the tuk-tuk takes off with Bastam inside.

- 76 s, 4:3, with no episode number. New `garage` set: lift, tyres, barrels, tool board, office corner and a big open door.
  Fighting-game intro cards introduce each new character. There is also a rage meter, a laptop screen, a fix bar and an "area unlocked" ending.
- `scene11.js` is `engine8.js` + `alamy.js` (El-Alamy's model) + `ep11.js`, with `${TUK}` replaced by `tuk_block.js` (the tuk-tuk from the street episodes).
- El-Gazzar's "addiction" is kept implicit (slow, "fixing his mood", "life is beautiful"). No drug imagery is shown, to stay within platform rules.
