# كاشف الكدب ٣٠٠٠ (Lie Detector 3000): low-poly cutscene source

Ibrahim's new invention is a pressure cooker with a TV remote taped on, a red beacon, and a kitchen colander as the "sensor helmet". It whistles whenever
someone lies. It catches Hamada's "I studied five hours", then Hamada turns it on Ibrahim over the missing basbousa. Om Hamada takes over the
interrogation, the cooker screams and its lid sticks to the ceiling. She ends with the unanswerable "am I prettier, or my sister?", and Ibrahim
walks himself out to the balcony.

- 61.5 s, 4:3, with no episode number.
- `scene9.js` is `engine8.js` (shared with `../el-diouf-gayeen-src`) plus `ep9.js`.
- Voices: Google AI Studio via the Claude-in-Chrome extension (Orus = Ibrahim, Puck = Hamada, Kore = Om Hamada, Algenib = announcer).
  The lines were mapped with a Gemini transcript (`../tools/gemini_transcribe.py`, model `gemini-3.5-flash` or `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`).
  Pitch alone was misleading on this take.
