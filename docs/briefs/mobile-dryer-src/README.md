# مجفف الموبايلات النووي (Nuclear Phone Dryer): vertical 9:16 source

- **Cold open:** dinner with guests (Abu Samir and Auntie Soad). The rice plate vibrates and glows: «إبراهيم! الرز بيرن!».
- **REWIND** ("قبلها بساعة..."), then the hook «لما الموبايل / يقع في الحمام».
- **The story:**
  - Hamada's phone drops into the toilet (splash).
  - Ibrahim unveils the **Nuclear Phone Dryer 3000**: the rice pot with a hair dryer taped on and a nuclear sticker. The phone goes in the rice, and the gas fire goes up.
  - Om Hamada takes the pot for the guests. At dinner the rice rings.
  - Hamada pulls the phone out: «اشتغل!... بس ريحته رز بالشعرية».
- **Ending:** PHONE STATUS: ALIVE, then "يُتبع...".

## Files
- `scene17.js` is `engine8.js` (W = 540, H = 960) + `vertical_common.js` + `ep17.js`.
- `sfx17.py` synthesises the music and SFX, then mixes them with ducking.
